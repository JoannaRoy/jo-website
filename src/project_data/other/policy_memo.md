This was a personal project in 2024. Of course, many social media regulations have been imposed since then, but at the time, platforms were largely unregulated (although some bills were being debated, including Australia's eventual age-restrictions). This was my first time writing a policy memo (or even being aware of this document format), so of course there are (many) aspects I would change if I re-wrote it now -- but I still found it to be an interesting project. As my first time exploring the social media <-> mental health landscape, it definitely sparked my interest in the overlap between these topics and is likely part of the reason I am still interested in AI <-> mental health, social media dynamics, and public policy today.
