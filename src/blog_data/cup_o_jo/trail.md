trail recently got acquired (woohoo!). We are now part of [Collibra](https://www.collibra.com/), a company which was founded ~18 years ago to focus on data governance, but which has recently moved into AI governance as well (which is where team trail will contribute going forward). 

In light of this, I have been feeling a bittersweet mix of nostalgia for the last couple years with trail and excitement for the future with Collibra. 

trail was a special company which I feel very (very) lucky to have been a part of —- it’s not often you get to work with such an ambitious, fun, tight-knit, passionate team of people. I can confidently say that coming to Munich to work with them is one of the best decisions I’ve made, and I’m really proud of what we accomplished. 

Of course, I know that much of this will also carry into our new chapter at Collibra, so I’m also excited for everything that will bring. Everyone has been really welcoming so far, and I know we will learn a lot from their team and all the work they’ve already done on both data and AI governance. 

![Trail memory 0](trail/photo-00.jpeg "team trail at a mountain hütte on our retreat last year! | w=320")

So anyways, in the midst of all these feelings, I tried to capture a bit of the nostalgia part with the following poem:

---

**trail 💙💚**

In Toronto, around two years back  
I was on my phone, scrolling through slack  
I should have been writing my thesis  
But instead, happened to see this:  
Engineering role at trail (full stack)  

The post was from a guy called ‘Nick’  
Intrigued, on the link I clicked  
Submitted my application  
And to my great elation  
It was me who they eventually picked  

In hindsight, this was a bit of a risk  
Moving continents can surely be mixed  
New language, new job, and new city  
Some parts of it were definitely tricky  
But trail-wise, things mostly just … clicked  

![Trail memory 1](trail/photo-01.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


The team was incredibly nice  
They helped me to start my new life  
Introduced me to brotzeit and beer,  
Pretzels, and all their friends here  
Plus, Nick even helped me move (twice)  

![Trail memory 2](trail/photo-02.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


Trail’s culture, I was also quite liking  
Playing padel, and Wizard, and hiking  
Pasta parties at lunch  
Pancakes for brunch  
And they even got me into biking!  

My shenanigans were always embraced  
Snowlafs, BINJOs, and Rubber Duck Cakes  
Few people have made me laugh more  
Even when I walked *into* the door  
Though, my nap room never did take shape … :(  

![Trail memory 3](trail/photo-03.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")


Of course, there were many hard moments  
Debugging, and late night deployments  
Last minute preparations  
Stressful demo presentations  
Not to mention all our visa appointments  

But I think mostly, it was everyone here  
Who made it so fun for these years  
I should especially thank Sven  
Who believed in me when  
I was a brand new software engineer  

See, Sven is a really good mentor  
He’ll joke: “You haven’t heard of \_\_\_\_ before?!?!”  
But all his tangents and PR reviews,  
Hard questions, and random tech news  
Taught me more than I could ever’ve asked for  

Later, team tech got Marketa  
And I am *so* glad to have met her  
We’ve had some very good talks  
And one-on-one walks  
Plus, tried a ferrata in questionable (?) weather  

![Trail memory 4](trail/photo-04.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")

![Trail memory 5](trail/photo-05.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")


So, team tech clearly made me better  
And some of our code – pretty clever!  
Just know I feel lucky  
For our matching duckies  
And the fact we’ll be staying together  

![Trail memory 6](trail/photo-06.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")


Next up is the business team  
Namely, Chefs Nick and Karim:  
Two peas in a pod  
Heads shaved, a little bit odd  
But working with them was supreme  

![Trail memory 7](trail/photo-07.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


See, Nick is not *just* the most German  
He’s also diligent and determined  
Manager of the office  
He brings in trail’s profits  
And has the best laugh (you’ve heard him)  

Karim is always there with a wink  
But, don’t bother making him a drink ☕  
He’s most in his zone  
Doing sales on the phone  
And now he’s gonna try his own thing  

Team product helped us know what to do  
(( lots of tickets for us to tend to 😄 ))  
Yogasai updated our content,  
We even shared an apartment :),  
And she always kept us up on the #news  

Nikolaus has a funny photo he despised  
Also, he onboarded me when I first arrived  
Welcomed me with a hug  
And, every day after, with a list of bugs  
But on his bike is where he really thrived  

![Trail memory 8](trail/photo-08.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


Then finally, there’s our CEO  
Top chef, one might say — the Anna to my Jo:  
A girlboss leader,  
And avid reader  
Whom I look up to more than she knows  

![Trail memory 9](trail/photo-09.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")

![Trail memory 10](trail/photo-10.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")


See, we’ve had our share of silly fights  
Should my desk be brown, or white?  
She’s *throwing* things away  
My salad dressing is NOT OKAY  
But I also know she gave (many) late nights  

Beyond this, there were more than a few  
Past trailies that got us here too  
Simon, Baris, Francis, and Lola  
Hendrik, Valentin, and Nicola  
And more still whom I never knew  

![Trail memory 11](trail/photo-11.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


Together, team trail is ambitious,  
Smart, driven, and judicious  
You’ve all left me inspired  
So, now that we’re acquired  
I hope you know that I will miss this 💙  

![Trail memory 12](trail/photo-12.jpg "| w=320 | align=left")


Yes, much will stay the same  
But there will, of course, be change  
So before we move on  
We should look back upon  
All that we’ve built along the way  

All the assets created  
Frameworks translated  
Content updated  
Documents generated  
Permissions complicated  
Versions upgraded  
Requirements stated  
RFCs debated  
Agents gated  
Asyncs awaited  
Sculptures afraided 🫣  

![Trail memory 13](trail/photo-13.jpg "| w=180 | align=left")


Team dedicated  
Sometimes frustrated  
Coffee appreciated  

![Trail memory 14](trail/photo-14.jpg "| w=120 | align=left")


So now that we've made it,  
Let's celebrate it! 💚
